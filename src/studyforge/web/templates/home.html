Turn your own notes into a study schedule that actually works.

How it works

Add a course, paste your notes or upload a PDF, and StudyForge extracts the text, splits it at meaningful boundaries, and finds the terms the material actually defines. From there you can generate flashcards and quizzes, or write your own — and every generated card links back to the exact passage it came from.

Real spaced repetition

FSRS-6 — the algorithm Anki ships — implemented in full and verified against the reference implementation across 4,096 review transitions. Scheduling is pure arithmetic; no language model touches it.

Works with no AI

Concept extraction, card generation, quiz generation and scheduling are all deterministic. AI is an optional enhancement, never a dependency, and the default is off.

Your notes stay yours

SQLite on your disk. No account, no sync, no telemetry. Nothing leaves the machine unless you switch on an AI provider, and the settings page tells you exactly what would be sent.

Honest about what it knows

A concept you have answered twice is marked not enough data, not “mastered”. Every status label has a written definition, and every percentage shows the sample it came from.

Traceable material

Generated cards and questions record the document and passage they came from, so you can always check the source rather than trusting the card.

Built for the keyboard

Space reveals, 1–4 rate, Esc leaves. A full review session never needs the mouse, on desktop or on a phone.
